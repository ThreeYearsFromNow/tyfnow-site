Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Three Years From Now Ltd runs this website. This page says what we collect, why, where it goes and how long we keep it. It's short because we don't collect much.

Who we are

Three Years From Now Ltd, company number 17013338, registered in England and Wales. Registered address: 259 Sheepcot Lane, Watford WD25 7DJ. Email: team@threeyearsfromnow.com. We're the data controller for everything on this page.

What we collect and why

If you write your goal on the front page and leave an email

We collect your email address, what you wrote, and the date you wrote it. We use it to email you what you wrote: the same day, then on the first, second and third anniversaries. Four emails. Nothing else. Lawful basis: your consent, given by ticking the box. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any of those emails or writing to team@threeyearsfromnow.com, and we'll delete what we hold and send nothing further.

If you write your goal but don't leave an email, nothing is sent to us. What you wrote stays in your browser's local storage on your device and never leaves it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

If you use the contact form

We collect your name, email, business name if you give it, and what you tell us is not working. We use it to reply to you and, if we end up talking about working together, as the start of that conversation. Lawful basis: your consent for the message, and our legitimate interest in answering an enquiry you made.

If you email us

We hold the email and reply to it. Lawful basis: legitimate interest in answering you.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and buttons get used. It records events (a page viewed, a button pressed), not what you typed. It only loads after you accept it on the notice that appears on your first visit. We have turned on IP anonymisation and turned off advertising features. Lawful basis: your consent, given on the cookie notice.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't use it to profile you or to train any system. We don't run advertising pixels or retargeting on this site. We don't add you to a newsletter unless you ask for one separately, and there isn't one yet.

Please don't send us health information or other sensitive personal details through the front-page field or the contact form. We don't need them and we'd rather not hold them. If you do, we'll delete them.

Where your data goes

  • Netlify (Netlify Inc., USA) hosts the site and receives form submissions. Transfers outside the UK are covered by Netlify's data processing agreement and the UK addendum to the standard contractual clauses.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC) provides analytics, and Google Workspace carries our email.

Nobody else sees it.

How long we keep it

  • Goal and email from the front page: three years and one month from the date you wrote them, then deleted. The extra month covers the third anniversary email.
  • Contact form messages and emails: two years from the last message in the conversation, then deleted, unless you've become a client, in which case our client terms apply.
  • Analytics: Google retains event data for 14 months, the shortest setting it offers.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to stop using it, or object to how we're using it. Email team@threeyearsfromnow.com and we'll answer within a month. There's no charge.

If you think we've got something wrong, tell us first and we'll fix it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. Google Analytics sets cookies only after you accept them on the notice that appears on your first visit. Decline and the site works exactly the same.

Local storage is used to remember whether you've answered the front-page question, what you wrote if you did, your cookie choice, and whether you've seen the leaving message. That data stays on your device.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Anything material and we'll say what changed.

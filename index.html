Three things that get you found

Brand

We're better than them, but I can't say why in a sentence.

Brand is that sentence, and the proof behind it. Without it, strangers pick on price or pick whoever they saw first.

Website

People visit. Nobody gets in touch.

The salesperson that never sleeps, if it's built to sell. It says the sentence, shows the proof, and asks for the enquiry. Most describe the services and stop.

Content

We post a lot more now. AI does most of it.

Content is why they remember you. Not how often you post, when every post looks like the last hundred they scrolled past. Fewer things, built from the sentence, that only you could have said.

That's where AI earns its place: doing the heavy lifting inside those three. Not making images and funny videos.

Build. Measure. Repeat. Until the number moves

We start with whichever of the three is losing you the most customers this quarter. Fix it, measure it, move to the next

Build

Something gets built, fixed or improved every week. One per cent better, every week, beats one big jump that never ships. Everything that gets built gets tracked.

Measure

So progress is structured. Measure the output and the results follow: enquiries, quotes, jobs won, this month against last, for a business like yours in your area. One page, every Friday.

Repeat

Learn, then repeat. What brings customers gets systemised. What doesn't gets dropped. Then something new gets tested, because the boundary of what works is meant to move.
